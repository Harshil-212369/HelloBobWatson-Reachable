# Hackathon Official Rules — Summary
**Source:** Dev Day Hackathon Official Rules PDF (do not re-read the PDF — read this file)  
**Event:** IBM TechXchange 2026 Pre-conference Dev Day Hackathon  
**Period:** 10:00 AM ET Aug 28 → 10:00 AM ET Aug 30, 2026  
**Scoring max:** 20 points | **Prize threshold:** minimum 12.5 points

---

## Scoring Criteria (4 × 5 points = 20 total)

| Criterion | Points | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Completeness and feasibility** | 5 | Does it work? Does the end-to-end solution hold up? |
| **Effectiveness and efficiency** | 5 | Does it actually solve the problem? Does it do it well? |
| **Design and usability** | 5 | Is the output clean, clear, easy to understand and use? |
| **Creativity and innovation** | 5 | Novel approach, not just a recombination of existing tools |

---

## Required Deliverables (all four are mandatory)

| # | Deliverable | Constraint |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Video demonstration | Max 3 min; min 90 sec of working solution; must show Bob usage; narrated; publicly accessible URL |
| 2 | Written problem + solution statement | ≤500 words; in submission form text field |
| 3 | Written statement on Bob usage | Specific and clear; names features; optionally mentions watsonx.ai / Orchestrate |
| 4 | Code repository + Bob session exports | Public GitHub/GitLab/Bitbucket; includes exported IBM Bob report of all relevant tasks/sessions |

**Key wording from the rules (verbatim):**  
> "Working code repository or evidence of technology proof-of-concept solution, including **exported IBM Bob report of all relevant tasks/sessions** used for the contest"

---

## Critical Constraints

- **No IBM Cloud credentials in the repo.** Detection = immediate account suspension.
- **All work must be done during the contest period** (Aug 28–30). No pre-built substantive solutions.
- **No post-deadline changes.** Changing video or repo after 10 AM = disqualification risk.
- Submissions must be in **English**.
- One entry per team. You can save draft submissions and resubmit until deadline.
- AI Submission Advisor emails feedback after first submit — read it and revise if needed.

---

## Key Interpretation: "Exported IBM Bob Report of All Relevant Tasks/Sessions"

The rules use the phrase **"all relevant tasks/sessions"** — not "one screenshot" and not "at the end."  
This means: **export after every meaningful Bob task session** throughout the build.  
A task session = one focused Bob conversation that accomplishes something (planning, implementing a check, writing the problem statement, etc.).

**Minimum expected exports:**
1. After planning session (this session)
2. After building Tier 1 (Families A+B)
3. After Tier 2 (Family C, if completed)
4. After writing problem/Bob statements

---

## What Makes a Winning Submission (from agenda, not rules)

- Solves ONE problem exceptionally well
- Demonstrates measurable impact (productivity, error reduction, time saved)
- Uses Agent Mode, Subagents/Parallel Tasks, Document Understanding
- Polished prototype, functional, intuitive
- Creative perspective + could realistically scale
